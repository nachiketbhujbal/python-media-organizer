## Decision

Reconcile current documentation in a separate documentation-only pull request.
Mark v0.7.1 released, retain exact review and publication evidence, and identify
v0.7.2 queue manifest/planning as the next promoted unit. The immutable release
tag remains on the implementation merge and is never moved or recreated.
No package behavior or release controls change in this reconciliation.

## Limits

Authoring supports complete retained-in-place, no-cache workflows. Future
managed-quarantine identities remain underivable and are refused; manually
reviewed schema-4 workflows retain their existing boundary. Accepted schema-5
policies require exact candidate and policy bindings plus separate private
receipts bound to their own and the policy's filesystem identities. Interrupted
or substituted artifacts never supply replacement authority automatically.

Production readiness and manual retirement remain separate decisions under
ADR 0109. No real collection, baseline, planner, cleanup or PyPI publication was
part of this release. Queue orchestration remains planned through v0.7.5.
